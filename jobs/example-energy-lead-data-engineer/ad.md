# Lead Data Engineer – Example Energy Oy (Helsinki, hybrid)

Example Energy Oy produces and sells electricity and district heating to 400 000 customers. Our data platform team builds the foundation for forecasting, trading, and customer analytics. We are now looking for a **Lead Data Engineer** to lead the technical direction of the platform and a team of four engineers.

## What you will do

- Lead the design and development of our Azure and Databricks lakehouse
- Build batch and streaming pipelines for metering, market, and weather data
- Set up and own CI/CD and infrastructure as code for the platform
- Coach and lead a team of four data engineers, and work closely with the analytics and trading teams
- Drive data governance and data quality practices

## What we expect

- At least 5 years of experience in data engineering
- Strong hands-on experience with Azure and Databricks (PySpark, Delta Lake)
- Excellent Python and SQL skills
- Experience in data modelling (dimensional modelling or Data Vault)
- Experience with CI/CD and infrastructure as code (e.g. Terraform, Azure DevOps or GitHub Actions)
- Experience in leading or mentoring engineers
- Fluent Finnish and English

## Nice to have

- Experience with streaming technologies (Kafka, Azure Event Hubs, Spark Structured Streaming)
- Background in the energy sector or with time-series data
- Unity Catalog or other data governance tooling
- Power BI
- Databricks or Azure data engineering certifications

## We offer

Hybrid work from our Helsinki office (2 days a week on site), a modern stack, and an important mission in the energy transition. Apply by 31 October 2026.
